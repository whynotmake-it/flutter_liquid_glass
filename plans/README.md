- **BUG-05 (direct mode exits after one static frame → jitter on stuttery

  paths)**: deferred, pre-existing. Plan 006's architecture may lift the
  16-shape cap entirely; noted in both 004 and 006 maintenance notes.
- **PERF-03 (backdrop pass covers union AABB including empty space)**:
  folded into Plan 006 (per-component passes are the candidate fix).
- **PERF-06 (transform-tracking layers walk `getTransformTo(null)` every
  composite)**: deferred; invalidation semantics are subtle and the win is
  unproven. Revisit after Plan 006 settles the architecture.
- **PERF-07 (direct path evaluates full sceneSDF per pixel)**: deferred
  into Plan 006 (shape-AABB culling / hybrid texture options evaluated
  there).
- **Benchmark coverage gap** (no multi-shape/blend/component scenarios in
  `benchmark_test.dart`): handled inside Plan 006 Step 1 rather than as a
  standalone plan.
