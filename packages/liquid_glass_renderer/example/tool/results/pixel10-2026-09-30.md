**#178, texture reuse: a large win on every geometry-rebuild frame.**
- **Frame rate.** Scenes that rebuild geometry go back to the vsync limit: button stretch 103.7 → 120.1 fps, pill 102.5 → 119.2, 5-pill blend 103.2 → 120.5, `resizeAnimated` 100.0 → 109.0, sheet 93.4 → 99.8, three layers 67.4 → 116.1.
- **UI thread.** Build p50 falls about 60% (button stretch 3.08 → 1.22 ms) and p95 12.4 → 2.9 ms. The `PAINT` slice falls 3.74 → 0.94 ms, and on three layers 9.43 → 2.18 ms. This removes the build-tail problem from sections 1 and 4.
- **GPU per frame.** Unchanged: Mcycles/frame are within ±2%. mJ/frame is 1–4% higher on the stretch scenes and 11% higher on three layers (5.88 → 6.54), because the governor clocks the GPU higher once it gets more frames.
- **Power.** Rail power follows frame rate. GPU mW goes up 20% on button stretch (251 → 305) and doubles on three layers (385 → 758, with 1.7× the frames). SoC mW is +4% on the single-shape scenes (812 → 844), +2% on the sheet and +34% on three layers (1169 → 1571). **Per drawn frame, SoC energy falls**: button stretch 7.8 → 7.0 mJ (−10%), three layers 17.3 → 13.5 mJ (−22%).
- **Memory and GC.** Peak PSS + GPU falls: sheet 816 → 629 MB (−23%), `resizeAnimated` 591 → 425 MB (−28%), 5-pill blend 309 → 248 MB (−20%). UI-thread GC falls from about 19 to 5 ms/s.

**#179, deferred submit: a smaller `PAINT` slice, but almost no change in UI build time; the verdict is marginal.**
- **`PAINT` slice.** About 45% shorter: 0.92 → 0.52 ms on single-pass scenes, 2.19 → 1.17 ms on three layers. The submit moves into `addToScene`, which also runs on the UI thread.
- **Build time.**
  - Beyond noise only on the multi-pass scene: build p50 2.13 (2.09–2.15) against 2.38 (2.32–2.39) ms, and p95 5.6 against 6.0 ms.
  - On single-pass scenes build p50 is within ±0.03 ms.
- **Unchanged.** fps, GPU Mcycles, mJ/frame, GPU mW, SoC mW (three layers: 768 vs 761 GPU mW, 1578 vs 1583 SoC mW) and memory are all within noise.
- **Against the PR's decision rule.** It gains about 0.25 ms of build p50 on multi-pass layers, and nothing on single-pass layers or on power.
