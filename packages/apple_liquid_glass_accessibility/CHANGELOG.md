## 0.1.0-dev.1

- Initial prerelease: `GlassPlatformTraits` feeds Reduce Transparency, Show
  Borders and Prefer Cross-Fade Transitions from iOS and macOS, and the
  Liquid Glass slider and the system's preference for reduced resource usage
  from iOS 27, into `GlassTraits`; `AppleAccessibilityFeatures.watch()`
  reports them.
